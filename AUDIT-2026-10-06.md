### 1.2 New in 40f1520: CPU auction bidders (`supabase/migrations/20261006000001_cpu_bidders_and_shop_customers.sql`)

- **1.2.1 HIGH (economy): Relisting rerolls the hidden ceiling, so the "gamble" can be farmed.** `cpu_ceiling` is drawn once per listing (`:84-90`), and the CPU bids only if `v_min <= cpu_ceiling` (`:95`). A seller who sets the starting bid at about 2× quicksell gets no CPU bid unless the 5% "collector" bucket (1.6-2.5×) or the top of the 15% bucket is rolled. The listing then expires with `bid_count = 0`, the cards come straight back (`:174-181`), and the seller relists for a fresh roll. Many parallel single-copy listings make this cheap. The result is a steady sale rate at ≥2× quicksell, not the advertised EV of ~1.06×. Fix (pick one or more): derive the ceiling from a stable seed (`hashtext(seller||card_id||date)`) so relisting doesn't reroll; charge a non-refundable listing fee; or make the CPU's chance of bidding fall as the starting price rises above its own valuation. **— FIXED in PR #162:** ceiling now seeded per seller/card/foil/UTC day (relisting draws the same one), and the CPU ignores auctions opening above quicksell.
- **1.2.2 MEDIUM: The 8-per-24h cap on CPU-won auctions isn't enforced.** The cap (`:97-99`) counts only listings already settled as `sold`, and it is checked when the CPU *bids*, not when the auction *settles*. The CPU can lead any number of the seller's concurrent auctions (up to 40 per pass), and all of them settle as CPU wins in `settle_expired_listings` (`:165-173`) without re-checking the cap. Failure: 30 auctions ending together → 30 CPU payouts. Fix: count CPU-led *active* listings plus CPU-won sales in the window, or re-check the cap at settle time and expire or return the over-cap ones. **— FIXED in PR #162:** cap re-checked at settlement; over-cap CPU wins return the cards.
- **1.2.3 MEDIUM (economy): CPU wins feed back into prices that Shop Floor customers pay.** *(Kept by design — the request was for CPU sales to count toward market pricing. The 1.2.1 fix removes the pump; the Shop Floor reference stays capped at 2× quicksell.)* CPU-won sales at up to 2.5× quicksell are recorded as `sold` with `current_bid` and count toward the market reference (header `:20-21`). The Shop Floor reference is `least(reference_price, 2×qs)` (`:252`), and customers then offer up to 1.4× that (`:300-305`). Pumping the market value with 1.2.1 lifts shop offers to about 2.8× quicksell. Fix: leave CPU-won sales out of `get_card_market_value`/`card_blended_reference`, or weight them down heavily.
- **1.2.4 LOW: The CPU doesn't recheck its target or tell the human it outbid.** `:107-109` refunds the outbid human silently: no notification row or activity entry. The Marketplace learns about it only on the next fetch. Fix: insert a notification the same way human-outbid does. **— FIXED in PR #162:** there is no server notification system for human outbids either; outbids surface through the Marketplace activity view, and the badge now reads "OUTBID BY <CPU name>".
- **1.2.5 LOW: CPU bidding only runs when someone browses.** `run_cpu_bidders` runs only inside `settle_expired_listings`, which only runs on a client fetch. With no traffic, no CPU bids happen; then a burst of traffic gives every listing one bid. Fix: run it from `pg_cron` every 5 minutes. **— FIXED in PR #162:** pg_cron installed; `settle_expired_listings()` (which runs the CPU bidders) is scheduled every 5 minutes.

### 3.2 CPU AI audit (`ai.ts`)

**Structure.** The AI is a greedy heuristic planner with no search. Its steps are: mulligan (`handIsKeepable`), wellspring choice, `mainPhasePlays` with a single reaction reservation, Leader ability, `chooseAttackers` (a clash-damage model that includes Doublestrike, first strike, Venomous and Unbreakable), `chooseGuards`, and `respondToStack` / `reactionPlays`.

**Quality is decent.** Invariant count is 0, colorClogged is 0, wellspringMisplay is 0, and P1 wins 46.2% (balanced seat).

**Measured lapses (one sim, 264 games):**
- `idleLeader` 105 (about 0.4 per game): the Leader ability is skipped when it was usable.
- `itemOnDoomedUnit` 105: Items are bonded to units that die that turn.
- `guardDiesForNothingDiscretionary` 25 and `tookGuardableLethal` 5. The second is the worst kind of error, a loss that could have been prevented.
- `reservationWasted` 47 of 139 reservations, a 34% waste rate. Holding back for a reaction still costs tempo about a third of the time.
- `wastedEssencePerGame` is 34.3 against 103.6 spent, so about 25% of essence is floated and lost. Part of this is structural, but it is high.
- `venomousSuicide` 95 (91 tagged deliberate). Check that "deliberate" really means a favorable trade.

**Exploitable weaknesses:**
1. **One reserved reaction card per turn**, chosen by `invokePriority` rather than by the threat on board. A player can bait it with a weak attacker and then commit the real threat.
2. **No lethal check across turns.** The attacker and guard models evaluate one clash. There is no "opponent's board kills me next turn" race calculation. This explains the 5 guardable lethals.
3. **The reservation is skipped when the opponent has no attack-capable unit** (`oppCanAttack`). Holding units back, then playing an Ambush or haste-style threat, means the AI never keeps an answer up.
4. **Deterministic targeting** (`chooseTarget`). The same board always gets the same answer, so a human learns to feed it decoys.
5. **Item bonding ignores pending removal** on the stack or on the opponent's open mana, which causes the 105 itemOnDoomedUnit lapses.

**Difficulty levels (none exist).** Proposed:
- **Easy:** a 25% chance to skip the best attack or block, no reaction reservations, random play order among affordable cards.
- **Normal:** the current AI.
- **Hard:** a 1-ply lookahead over the top 3 to 5 candidate main-phase sequences, scored by an evaluation function (board Might and Grit, Vitality differential, cards in hand, essence efficiency). Add a two-turn lethal/race check. Discount Item bonding on units below a "survives the opponent's best removal" threshold.
- **Expert:** a determinized Monte-Carlo pass (sample the opponent's hand from the remaining deck, 50 to 200 rollouts with the Normal policy) at main phase and guard declaration only.

Wire the difficulty choice into Settings and the match setup. Pay a reward multiplier on Hard and Expert, but only after the server can verify the difficulty (it is minted in match tickets).

**Concrete improvements, by expected gain:**
1. Add a lethal and race check to guards and attacks. This fixes tookGuardableLethal.
2. Make bonding removal-aware, discounting units the opponent can kill with visible essence.
3. Choose the reaction reservation by the threat profile of the opponent's board. Release it at end of turn into a main-phase play when the opponent did not attack. That play is impossible after the turn ends, so instead reserve only when the probability the opponent attacks next turn is high.
4. Add an idle-Leader fallback: use the ability whenever its EV is above 0, even if it is not optimal.
5. Pass an essence-curve check to the play sequencer: prefer the play set that maximizes essence spent (a knapsack over hand cost). This cuts the wasted 34/game.
6. Add seeded randomness (softmax over the top-k candidates) so the AI is less predictable.

### 3.3 Card balance and cost/stat formulas

**Formulas** (`cardpool.ts` ~l.940):
- Unit stat budget = `2*statBase + (roll 0..3) + statAdjust`.
- Might share is 0.62 for Ember/Gale, 0.38 for Root/Light, and 0.5 otherwise. Immobile caps Might at 30% of the budget.
- Keyword cost is a pure surcharge of about +1 cost per 2 weight (`KEYWORD_COST`).
- Items: `statBudget = t + 1 - survives - Tool`.

The ±3 stat-spread roll alone moves a card about 1.5 cost points. That variance is larger than most keyword weights, so many outliers are spread-roll outliers rather than keyword outliers.

**Leader win rates (pinned suite, n=432 each):**

| Leader | Win % | Spread |
|---|---|---|
| Avatar of the Abyss | 63.2 | 27.1 |
| Void Mother | 59.3 | 31.2 |
| Kuro | 56.3 | 33.4 |
| Mer-King | 54.2 | 31.3 |
| Sea Witch | 50.1 | 29.2 |
| Sovereign of the Dying Star | 45.0 | 44.8 |
| Legendary Diver | 43.5 | 45.9 (one deck at 10.4%) |
| Ruin-Walker Overseer | 43.2 | 18.8 |
| Sentinel of the Nether Pit | 40.3 | 25.0 |

The top-to-bottom gap is 23 points, and the two Void/Abyss Leaders top the table. Recommendations:
- Trim Avatar (one ability cost +1, or −1 to its aura) and Void Mother by about 5 points each.
- Buff Sentinel (random-deck 38.6% agrees with its pinned rate, so this is a real weakness). Use cheaper ability uses; it already has the highest uses per game at 8.28, so per-use value is low.
- Ruin-Walker has a low spread and a low median, so its kit itself is weak: give it +1 Grit on its aura or a cheaper invoke.
- Legendary Diver's 45.9 spread comes from one recipe at 10.4%. Fix that recipe before touching the kit.

**Keywords (carrier win %, from the sim):**
- **Overcosted / weak:** Overrun 39.3 (weight 3), Quickstrike 40.9 (weight 4), Fate 42.8 (0, cannot go lower, so it needs a rules buff), Bountiful 46.7, Soulbound 47.3.
  - Cut Overrun from 3 to 2 and Quickstrike from 4 to 3.
  - The Quickstrike band shows one card above the curve: Pulsating Clam, residual +19.7. So the keyword is underperforming while one stat-rolled carrier is overstatted. Fix the Clam's `statAdjust` (−2) and cut the keyword weight at the same time.
  - Fate needs a text buff: scry 2 instead of 1, or fate plus draw at 4+ Fate.
- **Undercosted / strong:** Freeze-Dry 68.9, Glaciate 66.7, Venomous 66.2 (weight 3), Empowering 60.0 (weight 3), Blighted 59.6 (weight 1).
  - Raise Venomous from 3 to 4 (its 95 suicides per sim show it trades up reliably).
  - Raise Blighted from 1 to 2 (Tethered Orbs is +16.2 flat).
  - Freeze-Dry and Glaciate: confirm the carrier counts first. If there are 5 or more, add +1 weight each.
- **Small-n keywords** (Bulwark 91.7% at n=12, Withering, Doublestrike): too few games to act on. Raise the sim count before pricing them.

### 3.4 Economy, grading and pack formulas under the new prices

**Quicksell:** C4 / U10 / R40 / SR120 / UR300 / FA500 / AA900 / M1500, foil ×2.5. The migration notes say pack EV is now about 59% of price (599cr) and a box about 75% (3799cr). These are healthy sink rates for a collection game.
- One issue: the box returning 16 points more than the pack makes boxes strictly better per credit, beyond the discount alone. That is acceptable but should be intended.
- The 1% Serialized extra pull is not covered by the quicksell numbers. Confirm the Serialized sell value is not a hidden EV spike.

**Levelling:**
- `xp_for_level = 20·L·(L−1)`. Level 20 needs 7,600 XP.
- Match XP is 60 for a win and 25 for a loss, so a player at 50% wins earns about 42.5 per match. Reaching the shop unlock takes about 179 matches, at roughly 15 min each about 45 hours. That is still long for a gate on a social feature. Suggest unlocking the shop at level 12 (2,640 XP, about 62 matches) or adding XP from daily quests. *(Unlock level: see §1.4.2 for the change that shipped.)*
- The level reward is 75cr per level, about 1,425cr total to level 20, worth about 2.4 packs. This is fine.

**Grading:**
- Multipliers run 1.1× (grade 5) to 4.5× (9), 6.5× (9.5) and 12× (10). Base fees are 400 / 180 / 70.
- Under the new quicksell, grading a Common (4cr) or Uncommon is always EV-negative: even a 10 gives 48cr against a 70cr fee. That is good as a sink, but the UI should show "expected return" per card.
- For a Rare (40cr), a 10 pays 480cr, so grading is EV-positive only if P(10) times 480 plus the rest exceeds the fee. This needs the roll table (`grading_roll` over 94) to be checked against the cheapest service. The risk is that the 70cr service plus a "generous" grade nudge turns Rares into a farm.
- Recommendations:
  - Either scale fees by rarity (for example 0.5 × quicksell plus a flat fee), or cap graded quicksell at about 3× the base for Common/Uncommon.
  - Re-derive the slab quicksell against the new table.
  - Make sure `crack_graded_slab` cannot be used to re-roll grades for free.

**CPU auction bidders:**
- Factor EV is about 1.06× quicksell, minus the 5% fee, which is about break-even. There are 8 CPU wins per 24h.
- The 10% tail at 1.6–2.5× is the farmable part. A player can mass-list Mythics (1,500cr) at the minimum and wait for the tail. The 8/day cap bounds this at about 8 × 1500 × 0.06 EV, roughly +700cr a day of expected surplus. That is acceptable. Confirm the cap is per account and not per listing, so alt-listing cannot bypass it.

**Shop Floor customers:**
- About 1 customer per 90 minutes. The reference price is capped at 2× quicksell and the walk-away price is 5–25% over the offer.
- The buy distribution peaks just above quicksell, so it works as a "slow quicksell plus a premium". This is fine.
- Watch combined income: shop plus bidders plus level rewards against the pack sink. Add a server-side daily credit-faucet total to telemetry.

**Shop costs:** setup 5,000cr, slot n costs `1500 + 750(n−1)`, upkeep 15cr per slot. Going from 4 to 30 slots costs 1,500 + ... + 20,250 = 282,750cr of collateral, which is unreachable for normal players. The slot step should be 250 instead of 750 (106,250cr total), or capped.

### 3.5 New archetypes and mechanics (7 essences: Ember, Tide, Root, Gale, Light, Shadow, Void)

**Archetypes (two-color):**
1. **Ember/Shadow "Pyre Sacrifice"** — Forfeit your own units for burst damage. Payoff: when a friendly unit goes to the Ash-pile, deal 1 to the opponent.
2. **Tide/Void "Erasure Tempo"** — bounce plus banish. Bounced enemy cards cost +1 next cast, and Void payoffs count banished cards.
3. **Root/Light "Sanctuary Wall"** — big-Grit Immobile units and lifegain. The win condition is Grit-as-damage clashes ("deals damage equal to Grit").
4. **Gale/Ember "Skyraid"** — cheap Aerial units with an Overrun payoff. Leverages the currently weak Overrun.
5. **Shadow/Root "Grave Bloom"** — Ash-pile recursion that fuels Root growth; each recursion gives +1/+1 counters.
6. **Light/Tide "Archive"** — Archivist / Echoing spell chains. The Event-centric deck needs the Event cost discount from 3.3.
7. **Void/Gale "Null Wind"** — deny the opponent's top-of-deck (mill/banish) to push the 4.5% deck-out win rate up into a real alternate win.
8. **Mono-color "Purist" shell** — payoffs that check "all your Wellsprings are one essence" (counterweight to the 2-color norm).

**New keywords/mechanics:**
1. **Kindle X (Ember)** — when played, if you dealt damage to the opponent this turn, it costs X less.
2. **Undertow (Tide)** — on a successful guard, return the attacker to its owner's hand.
3. **Overgrowth (Root)** — at Dawn, if you have unspent essence from last turn, add a +1/+1 counter. Turns wasted essence into value.
4. **Slipstream (Gale)** — can attack the turn it is played if you control another Aerial.
5. **Vigil (Light)** — the first time each turn a friendly unit would die, this unit may exhaust to prevent it.
6. **Echo of Ash (Shadow)** — may be cast from the Ash-pile for its cost +2, then banished.
7. **Collapse X (Void)** — at end of turn, banish X cards from the top of the opponent's deck. Gives Void a deck-out plan.
8. **Attune (any)** — gains a keyword based on the essence you paid with (Ember gives Quickstrike, Gale gives Aerial, and so on). This rewards mixed mana.
9. **Pact (Shadow/Void)** — pay Vitality instead of essence, 2 Vitality per point.
10. **Convoke-style "Rally" (Light/Root)** — exhaust untapped units to pay 1 generic each.
11. **Bounty (any)** — when this unit dies, the opponent draws a card. A drawback keyword that funds stats above budget (negative weight), adding tension to the cost formula.

### 3.6 Possible rule changes

1. **Essence carry-over of 1 floating point** (or a "Reservoir" cap of 1). This cuts the 34 wasted essence per game and reduces the AI's reserve-for-reaction tempo penalty (34% of reservations wasted).
2. **Defender-advantage tiebreak.** Seat balance is 46.2% P1, so P2's double-Wellspring opening slightly overcompensates. Try giving P2 1 Wellspring plus a card instead, and test at 48–52%.
3. **Leader shatter stakes.** Only 2 shatters occur in 264 games, so shattering is nearly irrelevant. Lower the Leader's durability, or let lethal damage to it count as Vitality damage, so attacking the Leader becomes a real line.
4. **Deck-out softening.** Deck-out decides 4.5% of games, and the loser's deck averages 36.3 cards remaining, so deck-out is not a pressure factor. Either drop it as a loss condition (fatigue damage instead) or embrace it via Void (3.5).
5. **Mulligan** (11.7% rate): adopt a "London" style mulligan (draw 7, bottom 1) for consistency.
6. **Slow Events gain "Quick for +1 essence"**, a universal rule that closes the Event residual gap.
7. **Game-length cap.** 30 games (11%) went past turn 30. Add a sudden-death erosion from turn 25 (erode +1 per turn) to keep sessions under about 20 minutes.

## 4. Replayability and deck-building creativity, plus new single-player collecting mini-games

### 4.1 Replayability gaps
1. **The core loop is closed to most accounts.** While `cpuLocked` is on, `games_played` and `wins` missions cannot progress for non-creators, so any match-based mission is dead content. The first replayability fix is ungating CPU play, at least at one difficulty.
2. **No variety of match conditions.** Every CPU match is the same format. Cheap variety can come from rule modifiers applied at `begin_match`, chosen server-side so they are recorded on the ticket:
   - Singleton: every card in the deck has 1 copy.
   - Two-colour max.
   - Commons/uncommons only ("Pauper").
   - Leader-locked weekly: everyone plays the same Leader.
   - Starting-hand mulligan variants.
3. **There is no reason to build a second or third deck.** Missions should reward using a deck *different from the last 3 played* (a deck hash on the ticket), winning with each colour, and winning with a deck that contains a newly pulled card.
4. **Puzzles.** Fixed board states built from `src/game/v3/engine.ts` fixtures (the test suites already construct such states) where the goal is "win this turn". They are deterministic, cheap to author and replayable for stars.

### 4.2 Deck-building creativity
- **Deck challenges**: "build a deck under N total rarity points", "build a deck with zero Rares", "build around today's Card of the Day". These are validated client-side through `deckAdvice.ts` and server-side at `save_deck` or on the match ticket.
- **Archetype tags and shareable deck codes with a "copied N times" counter**, which extends `deckcode.ts` and `DeckLinkPreview.tsx`.
- **Goldfish score**: `drawTestHand` already exists. Add a "curve consistency" score from 1,000 seeded test hands (pure client-side), shown as a grade in the builder.
- **Budget-fork button**: suggest owned substitutes for missing cards in an imported deck. This joins `deckcode` to the collection and colour/cost data.

## 7. Larger features and next steps (prioritized roadmap)

**P0: unblock the loop (weeks 1-2)**
1. **Open CPU play to all accounts.** The mission and achievement economy around matches cannot work without this. Ship difficulty tiers, and keep the reward caps in `record_match_result`.
2. **Stat-instrumentation migration.** This is quick win 2, plus a documented registry of `stat_key` values in a code comment or table so designers can author missions without touching SQL. Also add a CI test (`supabase/migrations.test.ts`) that every `achievements.stat_key` and `missions.stat_key` value is written by some `track_stat` call.
3. **Seed the Section 5 content,** with anti-farm rules: counted only from accepted tickets, account age gates, and price floors on trades and sales.

**P1: retention (weeks 3-6)**
4. **Match tickets carry the deck hash, colours and modifiers.** This enables per-colour and per-deck missions, deck-variety rewards and the rule modifiers in Section 4.1.
5. **Mini-games framework.** Build one `minigame_runs` table plus `start_minigame` and `act_minigame` RPCs with a server-held seed and per-day limits. Ship the Rarity Ladder, Archive Hunt and Appraiser first, since they need no engine work.
6. **Set Completion Bingo and set or colour completion tracking.** This ties collecting to rewards.
7. **Server-side wishlist,** feeding bingo, mini-game bonuses and marketplace alerts ("a wishlisted card was listed").

**P2: depth (weeks 6-12)**
8. **Puzzle mode,** using the engine fixture states.
9. **Pack Draft Gauntlet,** reusing ticketed matches and a tickets currency from missions.
10. **ELO CPU gauntlet and weekly modifier ladder** (already in ROADMAP "Next"). Add a seasonal leaderboard through `get_cards_leaderboard`-style RPCs.
11. **Match history and replays** (already in ROADMAP). These are needed to verify in-match feat achievements.

**P3: foundation (later)**
12. **Server-authoritative engine and PvP.** Once this exists, in-match feats can pay currency, and ranked missions become possible.
13. **Seasonal mission tracks tied to the battle pass,** where BP tiers unlock mission chains (multi-step quest lines with story text).

**Metrics to watch:** D1/D7 retention, missions claimed per daily active user, the share of mission rewards claimed by accounts younger than 72 hours (a farm signal), and how much credit enters the economy through mini-games compared with what leaves through sinks (grading, mystery packs, quicksell).

## Appendix A. Inventory used by §4–§7

- **Missions** are data-driven: `get_missions` / `claim_mission` RPCs (`src/lib/supabase.ts` ~L661-687). Each row has `stat_key`, `target`, `cadence: 'daily' | 'weekly'`, and rewards in `reward_credits`, `reward_vouchers` and `reward_bp_xp`. `AchievementsScreen.tsx` shows tabs for daily, weekly and achievements.
- **Achievements** are rows in the `achievements` table (`stat_key`, `target`, `category`, credits, vouchers, `reward_pack_id`) plus `player_achievements` (progress, claimed). They are claimed through `claim_achievement`.
- **Stat tracking**: a server function `track_stat(uid, key, amount, is_max)` writes to `player_stats`. The repo shows these keys being written: `games_played`, `wins`, `level` (max-type) from `record_match_result`, and `serialized_pulls`. String literals suggest `packs`, `cards` and `trades` too. The base schema is not in `supabase/migrations/` (only 9 recent migrations are there), so I could not confirm the full key list. **Every "Hook" below that names a key other than those 4 confirmed keys needs a `track_stat` call added to the named RPC.**
- **Other loops**: daily login streak (`claim_daily_login`, `profiles.login_streak`), Daily Free Pack (`claim_daily_pack`), Daily Bounties (`get_daily_bounties`, `buy_bounty_card`, `sell_bounty_card` at 5x quicksell, 1 per card and 3 per day), Card of the Day (`cardOfTheDay.ts`, which is client-side and seeded by `utcDayKey`), battle pass (`claim_bp_tier`), grading, mystery packs, player shops, auctions (`place_bid`), trades, friends, showcase, card submissions and the deck builder. The deck builder has deck codes, goldfish test hands (`goldfish.ts` has `seededShuffle` and `drawTestHand`) and `deckAdvice.ts`. Wishlist is localStorage only.
- **Modes**: CPU match (`begin_match` then `record_match_result` with a server ticket, plus caps and a duplicate guard). CPU play is currently **gated to the creator role for accounts** (`MainMenu.tsx` `cpuLocked`), while guests get a random-deck quick match. There is no PvP, no gauntlet and no draft.
