## 3. Settings, CPU solver, balance, formulas, rules

### 3.1 Settings
- Every stored preference is consumed (speed, hand sort, motion, difficulty). `hide_serialized_announcements` is written by RPC but not read anywhere in `src/`; check that the server-side feed filters on it.
- **The HARD description is false.** `matchPrefs.ts:199-203` says "Plans two turns of combat"; the code (`ai.ts:1062-1088`) is a single chump-block heuristic.
- Difficulty doesn't change rewards, so the optimal play is to farm Easy. Scale rewards (Easy ×0.6, Hard ×1.3) and record difficulty in match history.
- Missing settings: sound/volume (no audio exists), auto-pass / "stop for responses", end-turn confirm, colour-blind essence glyphs, text size, first-player choice for practice, a per-match difficulty picker. Consider `playable` as the default hand sort for new players.

### 3.2 CPU solver (`src/game/v3/ai.ts`)
- **Architecture:** one greedy pass with no search. Cards are prioritised roughly by cost×10; attacks and blocks are decided per unit. A turn takes 0.15–0.18 ms (p99 <1 ms), so there is plenty of time budget unused.
- **Measured strength** (±95% CI, 800 games each): easy vs normal 26.6%; **hard vs normal 50.9% (no stronger)**; hard vs easy 74.8%. HARD's two changes cancel out: it reserves reactions even when the opponent has no attackers (28.5% of reservations wasted), and it makes extra chump-blocks. `difficulty.test.ts:68-74` locks in this "hard > 45%" non-result.
- **Exploitable patterns:**
  1. The damage targeting in §1.2 E8. This is why Legendary Diver (ping kit) is the worst Leader at 34–40%, and why Blood Moon Descent rates −11.
  2. Attack evaluation assumes every attacker meets the same worst blocker (`ai.ts:855-866`). One big human blocker freezes the CPU's whole team.
  3. No crack-back model: it never considers that its attackers can't guard next turn.
  4. The mulligan only asks for "≥1 card costing ≤2 and ≥1 Unit". Hands averaging under 2.5 cost win 55.5% vs 47.7% at 3.5+.
- **Recommendation:** make HARD a real search. Roll out each candidate play+attack line 1–2 turns using the greedy policy as playout, with 50–200 rollouts (<50 ms). At minimum, do 1-ply attack-set search with blocker allocation. Fix E8 for every level, and the E3 tap-out too.

### 3.4 Formulas and economy
1. **Match income** (see §1.1 B4): 100 cr/win with a client-reported result and a 200/day cap ≈ 20k cr/day scripted, or ~3,200 cr/h from instant concedes at 40 cr per 45 s. Cap paid wins per day with diminishing returns, and scale by difficulty (§3.1).
2. **Grading is a guaranteed faucet.** The expected grade multiplier is 3.84 (4.61 for Keeper), so Keeper on a plain Rare is +74 cr EV, a Super-Rare +363, and a Mythic foil +13k (`grading.ts:76,103,159-203`). This undoes the quicksell rescale. Graded *quicksell* should be ≈ quicksell × (0.5 + 0.1 × grade), with the high multipliers kept for player-to-player sales only.
3. **Shop-customer trade ladder:** a target of ref × U(0.7, 1.3) with ref up to 2× quicksell lets UR → FA/AA → Mythic, up to 8 a day. Cap the target at the listing's rarity (see §1.1 B6). CPU auction bidders check out (EV ≈ 1.06× quicksell before the 5% fee, so not a farm).
4. **XP curve** `20·L·(L−1)` with a flat 75 cr per level: late levels feel empty. Add milestone packs at L10/25/50 and cap credit rewards around L100.
5. **Battle pass:** 2 wins per tier, so it inherits #1. Season 2 data must exist before 2026-11-10.
6. **Rarity power creep:** Rare+ units always get an effect and more keywords, so with copy caps higher rarity is mildly pay-to-win. Consider sidegrades at the top rarities.

### 3.5 Rule changes (data-backed)
- **Sanctums enter exhausted.** 1–2 cost blank Sanctums run +13 to +17 residual; Locations are the best card type.
- **Essence sink "Channel":** once per turn, pay 4 to deal a card. About 28.7 essence is wasted per game, 75% of it with an empty hand.
- **Free first mulligan** (draw 7, bottom 1). Mulliganing currently costs ~2.4 pp.
- **First-player compensation overcorrects** (P1 47%, and only 32.7% in ≤10-turn games). Move the shallow spring's expiry from turn 6 to turn 5 and re-measure.
- **Simultaneous zero Vitality is a draw** (closes E6).
- Give the two Shadow/Void Leaders distinct pairs (e.g. Void Mother → Void/Light).

### 3.7 New mechanics / keywords

- **Undertow** (Tide): on enter, return an enemy unit costing ≤2 to hand. Tide's "bounce" identity has no implementation yet.

## 4. Replayability, deck-building creativity, single-player mini-games

*Prerequisite:* CPU play must be open to accounts (§1.3 M1). Most items also need a match-metadata argument on `record_match_result` (`p_meta jsonb`: mode, difficulty, leader, colours, turns, final vitality, deck hash, seed). This drives missions only, not payouts, so client trust is acceptable when capped.

### 4.1 Modes
1. **Sealed vs CPU.** Open 6 virtual boosters (not granted), build 40 cards in `DeckBuilderScreen` with an `allowedPool` prop and `DECK_MIN=40`, then best of 3. Prizes scale with wins; optionally keep 1 card.
2. **Draft.** Pick 1 of 3 ×30, seeded from `packodds.ts`; same 40-card build.
3. **Roguelike Gauntlet.** 7 CPU nodes of escalating difficulty and fixed archetypes. Between nodes pick a boon: +3 starting Vitality (the existing `startVitality` hook), an extra opening Wellspring, or swap 3 cards. Server `gauntlet_runs` with a ticket per node.
4. **Deck Challenges.** Pauper, Mono-colour, Highlander, No-Units, Budget (<600 quicksell), Type-locked. Pure validators next to `validateDeck`; badges on decks.
5. **Rotating Weekly Format.** A `formats` table holding a rules JSON; "This week's format" on PlayScreen.
6. **Puzzle Mode.** `createPuzzle(spec)` next to `createGame`; JSON puzzles in `src/game/puzzles/`; first-clear rewards.
7. **Deck of the Week vs CPU.** A curated deck code with a fewest-turns leaderboard (match history already stores turns).
8. **CPU Ladder.** 10 ranks, each with a fixed difficulty and archetype; rank stored on `profiles`; rewards on rank-up.
9. **Leader Mastery.** 5/15/40 wins per leader unlock banners, giving 14 parallel progression tracks.
10. **"Build With" the Card of the Day.** The builder already receives it; win with it in the deck for a daily bonus.

### 4.2 Collecting mini-games
1. **Grade Guess.** Guess a raw card's grade band using `GRADE_ODDS`; 5/day; teaches the grading odds.
2. **Essence Memory.** A 4×4 flip grid of your own cards; match by essence or rarity; daily capped server claim.
3. **Pack Price Is Right.** Guess the quicksell total of the pack just opened; within 10% pays a bonus.
4. **Collector's Requests.** CPU personas (reusing the shop-customer personas) ask for e.g. "3 Tide Uncommons + 1 foil Rare" and pay 1.5× quicksell + BP XP. Reuses the bingo cell predicates.
5. **Showroom Appraisal.** Weekly CPU judging of the slab showcase (grade multiplier × rarity) with tier payouts.
6. **Hot Pull Wheel.** A daily hot essence and rarity; pulling a match pays a bonus (hook in `grant_pack_contents`).

## 7. Supabase backend: load and cost

### 7.1 What the live project shows
- **Scale today:** DB 18 MB, 4 auth users, 297 cards, Supabase Storage **empty** (all 297 card images already served from `frycards-art.pages.dev`; pack and shop rows contain no `supabase.co` URLs). Egress is no longer a storage problem.
- **Realtime is the dominant DB cost.** The WAL-polling query `realtime.list_changes` has **~363k calls / 2,122 s** of total DB time, more than everything else combined. There were 361 websocket connects in 24 h with ~1 active user, and `realtime.subscription` shows 7,426 inserts / 7,426 deletes (channel churn). Twelve tables are in `supabase_realtime`, including high-write `player_cards` and `market_listings`.
- **Storage lookups** (1.17M bucket and ~1.16M object lookups) are historical; storage logs show ~5 requests/day now.
- **Cron:** a single job, `settle_expired_listings` every 5 min (~11 ms). `cron.job_run_details` has no cleanup.

### 7.2 Client-side request reduction (ranked by savings)
1. **Marketplace refetch storm** (`src/lib/supabase.ts:1158`, `MarketplaceScreen.tsx:136`). Every list fetch first calls the *write* RPC `settle_expired_listings` (which also runs `run_cpu_bidders`). On top of that, an unfiltered `market_listings` subscription triggers a full 5–6 request reload, and `run_cpu_bidders` writes `cpu_next_at` on up to 40 rows every cron tick, which fans out a realtime event to every viewer. → Drop the client settle call; move `cpu_next_at` / `cpu_ceiling` to a side table (also closes §1.1 B5); patch rows from `payload.new` instead of reloading; select explicit columns.
2. **Double refresh after every action.** `MetaContext.tsx:353-361` subscribes to profiles, player_cards and player_inventory, *and* ~59 call sites refresh manually. One pack open costs ~9 requests where 1–5 would do. → Apply the RPC return values (`OpenPackResult` etc.) to local state, suppress realtime refresh for ~2 s after a local mutation, and cache `serialized_supply`.
3. **MainMenu refetches on every visit** (`MainMenu.tsx:217`): `get_missions` + the full static `achievements` catalog + `player_achievements`. → Cache the catalog in `cachedFetch` (30-minute TTL); refresh the badge only after claims, matches or realtime events.
4. **Shop Floor polling a write RPC** (`PlayerShopsScreen.tsx:3336, 3208`). `get_shop_customers` runs every 3 min even in background tabs, and every call runs `spawn_shop_customers` with a `FOR UPDATE` lock. → Use `setTimeout` to `next_at`, a visibility-aware interval, an early return server-side when nothing is due, and a read-only badge RPC.
5. **Per-card mystery stats polling** (`PlayerShopsScreen.tsx:1228`): 60 s per visible card (600 RPC/h for 10 cards) on top of an existing realtime subscription. → Drop the interval.
6. **Unfiltered realtime channels:** `player_shops` + `shop_listings` (`PlayerShopsScreen.tsx:955`), `friendships` + `trades` (`SocialScreen.tsx:212`), `serialized_supply` (`NewsCenterScreen.tsx:110`). → Add `filter: recipient=eq.<uid>` etc., multiplex into one channel per screen, and use visible-interval polling for directories. Also find and fix the subscribe/unsubscribe churn (7.4k cycles) — most likely channels re-created on re-render because their effect dependencies change.
7. **Catalog and boot.** The full `cards.template` re-download every 6 h → check a `catalog_version` row and lengthen the TTL to days. Seven boot reads → one `get_bootstrap()` RPC.
8. **Uncached RPCs:** `card_blended_reference` per bundle item (batch it), `get_card_market_value` per inspector open (5–10 min memo).
9. **Images bypassing `mediaUrl()` resizing:** `StoreScreen.tsx:424,512,608,931`, `BattlePassScreen.tsx:115,135`, `MainMenu.tsx:389`, `PlayerProfileModal.tsx:130,141`, `NewsCenterScreen.tsx:322`, `PlayerShopsScreen.tsx:362`. This now costs Pages bandwidth and mobile data rather than Supabase.

**Estimate:** a 30-minute session is ~230–300 requests today; items 1–3 cut that to ~60–90 (−65–75%).

### 7.3 Server-side
- **Remove high-churn tables from the realtime publication** where a filtered or polled alternative exists (`player_cards`, `market_listings`, `player_shops`, `shop_listings`, `serialized_supply`). Each change is WAL-decoded and RLS-checked once per subscriber, which is what drives the WAL-polling load in §7.1.
- **`get_bingo`** runs 25 separate `bingo_cell_done` scans of `player_cards ⨝ cards` and calls `card_sell_price()` per row → aggregate the collection once in a CTE.
- **`grant_pack_contents`** works per card: an `ORDER BY random()` scan, a `select *` including the `template` JSON, and one upsert each. A 24-pack open is ~240 of each. → Pre-bucket ids by rarity once per call, select only the needed columns, and bulk `insert … on conflict`.
- **`run_cpu_bidders`** runs a per-row `count(*)` by seller → index `market_listings(seller, status, ends_at)` or compute counts once. Add an early exit to the cron when there's nothing active or due.
- **Advisors:**
  - 6 RLS policies call `auth.uid()` per row (`player_wishlist` ×3, `bingo_cards`, `graded_cards`, `shop_customers`). Wrap them as `(select auth.uid())`.
  - 4 unindexed foreign keys (`graded_cards.card_id`, `player_wishlist.card_id`, `shop_customers.listing_id`, `shop_customers.offer_card_id`). `listing_id` is used in a `NOT EXISTS`, so index that one.
  - 43 unused indexes: keep them for now; with 4 users the stats are meaningless.
  - Drop `cards_art_backup_20261006` and `pack_types_art_backup_20261006` (no primary key, no policies).
- **Housekeeping:**

  - Add a daily `delete from cron.job_run_details where end_time < now() - interval '7 days'`.
  - Enable leaked-password protection.
  - Switch the Auth connection pool to a percentage.
- **Security posture:** all `admin_*` / `creator_*` RPCs call `assert_creator()`, and internal helpers (`track_*`, `spawn_*`, `bingo_*`, `run_cpu_bidders`) are revoked from `authenticated`. Remaining: revoke `settle_expired_listings` (§1.1 B7). `get_showcase_slabs` is anon-executable; that is fine only if public showcases are intended.
