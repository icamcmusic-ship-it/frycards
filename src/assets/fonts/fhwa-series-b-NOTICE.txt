FHWA Series B (fhwa-series-b-latin.woff2, fhwa-series-b-wide-latin.woff2)
=========================================================================

Used for: the Ultra-Rare "Contraband Crate" type line (FHWASeriesB2020plus0)
and its cost tag (FHWASeriesB2020plus50, the heavier cut).

Licence: public domain. The font's name table (nameID 0) reads "Public
domain outline and width data from the Federal Highway Administration's
Standard Highway Signs manual."

Being public domain, both are subset to printable ASCII and converted to
woff2:
  pyftsubset FHWASeriesB2020plus0.otf --unicodes=U+0020-007E,U+2019
    --flavor=woff2 --layout-features=kern,liga --no-hinting
  pyftsubset FHWASeriesB2020plus50.otf --unicodes=U+0020-007E
    --flavor=woff2 --layout-features=kern,liga --no-hinting
